Source server: serve repository root, visit SPINOFF_SOURCE/index.html. Save key `silicon-handset-v020`, schema2/release0.2.0, separate from Company saves. ## Implemented causal loop

Each seed generates4–8 rival brands, twelve supplier firms (two per component role) and the observed company, varying cash, team sizes, paid opening stocks/resources, core profiles and specialist knowledge. All start with feature-phone technology. No rival capability is computed from the player's capability, cash, victories or losses. Rival progress can diverge strongly because it depends on its own team, funds, inputs and work. Real market competition and recruiting may legitimately affect another firm's resources; the no-catchup test isolates these interactions rather than banning them.

Persistent named people reuse the unchanged Company HumanCore: personality, values, aptitudes/potential and needs. Their hourly sleep/meal/commute/work/rest schedule, condition and phase-specific subjects influence available work/learning. Daily wages and paid meals transfer real cash. Research, prototype validation, assembly and specialist roles use relevant knowledge; 15% of paid brand work is reserved for commercial work, multiplied by sales knowledge; each retail conversion consumes 25 effective sales-work units, leaving less time for engineering. This first version does not recreate every parent-game relationship/career feature.

Suppliers consume finite processed input units into persistent WIP and produce actual component stock. Paid upstream orders have shipping delays. Brands pay suppliers, wait for specific generation parts, consume real prototype parts, validate, procure batches, use paid EMS tooling, work through pilot/assembly/release and sell from finished inventory to finite household cash. Failed validation requires paid timed redesign and a new prototype. Material audits reconcile raw stock, transit, consumed material, prototype losses, WIP, finished components/phones and sales. Paid opening stock is explicit; no invisible recurring subsidy/refill.

R&D uses shared paid work and lab/tooling cost with cash-reserve gating. CPU/parts/OS measurements reuse phone engineering: GHz/cores, RAM/storage, camera, battery/thermal and task scores. Designs differ by supplier/technology/seed. Component-specific bespoke R&D and sophisticated strategic portfolios remain future work; knowledge mostly influences effort rather than unrestricted hardware invention.

Companies can recruit unemployed specialists or poach an employed engineer through autonomous salary offers, personal acceptance, notice and post-move cooldown. Transfers preserve identity/profile/subjects, remove former employer capacity and add it to the new team. The observer influences conditions through logged cash/individual subject/trait experiments, not a required manual hire button.

## API and invariants

UMD `SiliconSim` / CommonJS `SPINOFF_SOURCE/js/sim.js`: create(seed,{role}), advance(world,minutes), snapshot(world), command(world,c), serialize/deserialize, audit(world). `audit` returns `{ok,errors}`; `command` returns true or throws. Monetary values are integer satang. Snapshots clone state and do not mutate RNG/time. Advance retains minute-level display but settles only fully elapsed hours;60minutes equals12calls of5minutes. Save loads validate references/ownership/process requirements/material reservations, reject duplicate WIP and refreeze profiles.

World arrays: firms, people, designs, shipments, WIP, offers, events. Snapshot also exposes wip alias. Company stocks use kind:generation keys; designs retain measured chip/parts/evaluation and actual inventory/produced/sold counts. People carry stable employer IDs/subjects/core/state. Audit checks money total against opening+logged experiment capital and physical conservation. Startup stocks are paid opening endowments, distinct from free normal output.

## Explicit limits

This is the first playable vertical slice, not the whole Empire blueprint. Work is hourly; rendered campus is an activity diagram, not the original full pathfinding campus. Seven business roles and three bounded research increments; no global player-relative locks, but content is finite. Upstream uses aggregate processed-input units and paid tooling; it is not a detailed mine/refinery/wafer-fab simulation or realistic industrial NRE calibration. No multi-country politics, carrier finance, patent litigation, full app store, keynote/media ecology, extensive warranty/repair economy or complete social/career system. Consumers are three finite-wallet cohorts with different price limits, demand and usage preferences, not individual consumer lives. No physical-phone benchmark or mobile-device FPS certification.

## v0.2.0 commercial model and actual APIs

Three consumer cohorts own separate initial wallets: value THB5,000,000, photography THB4,000,000 and performance/apps THB3,000,000. `households.cash` is their aggregate, not additional money. Each purchase reduces both the buyer cohort and aggregate by the same actual price; money transfers to the seller. Price ceilings are THB1,900 / 3,300 / 5,200. Daily intents are 5 / 4 / 3 plus seeded0–2; unmet intent expires. There is no daily cash refill. Preferences use measured call, battery, low-light photo, web/game scores and price; performance buyers require web score8. Segment settlement order rotates daily. A sale needs released inventory, a funded buyer and 25 sales-work units. Sales work uses15% of the same paid work budget with multiplier0.25 + subject.sales/100; unused daily outreach expires. Sales knowledge practices only while working. No guaranteed sale is created by skill alone.

`reviewStrategy` (internal) runs at first daily settlement and then on a firm-staggered seven-day schedule. It evaluates all cohorts using funded demand, competitors' actual target plans, CEO traits and seed-specific commercial preferences. Each option selects an affordable generation bounded by the firm's own research and qualified suppliers. `strategy` stores options, selected segment/level, review time and a factual reason. New projects follow that target; ongoing projects retain their design. Finished-stock accumulation blocks new investment and reduces the next reviewed markup; price floors use estimated component/tooling/labor allowance, not an audited per-unit profit ledger. Designs retain `targetSegment`, `segmentSales`, `estimatedUnitCost`, `priceReason`. Weekly retargeting is a bounded heuristic, not perfect forecasting or a full portfolio optimizer.

`SiliconSim.supplierQuotes(world,buyerId,kind,level)` returns a pure sorted list with sellerId/key/stock/unitPrice/leadMinutes/score. Independent suppliers have seeded price terms (80–124.99% of the generation base) and12–72hour transit; low stock adds12%. NPC buyers weigh price against delay using CEO patience, purchase affordable available quantities, split shortages across offers and reserve paid physical inventory in shipments. The supplier debits stock immediately; the buyer cannot consume until delivery. `firm.procurement` stores the last evaluated alternatives/orders/unfilled need. Prototype procurement stops after input reservation, avoiding unnecessary replacement purchases during prototype work. Supplier quotes vary by stock, not by player fortune.

`SiliconSim.consumerUtility(design,segment)` is a pure inspection/test helper; only internal daily market settlement changes sales. UI shows cohort budgets/results, target/review reasons, per-model segment sales and procurement alternatives. No operational buy/price/target command is exposed to players. Existing create/advance/snapshot/command/serialize/deserialize/audit contracts remain, with schema2. Audit adds cohort wallet/purchase reconciliation, generation quote ownership, commercial work/terms/plans and observation-record shape checks. Old0.1.0 saves are explicitly rejected; their storage key and Company keys are untouched. Invalid current autosave is preserved and automatic saving paused until a successful explicit import/new world. Never overwrite the active world on invalid import.

Standardized-grade limit: inventory remains kind:generation, and measured specifications are shared within each generation from a qualified producer. Competing suppliers differ in economics and delivery, not yet in serial-level physical quality; the UI states this. Six extra suppliers have their own paid opening resources, capital and personnel. Four–eight independent rival brands remain seed-generated. Neither research nor strategic scores use observed-company power as a catchup input. The full original blueprint remains unfinished.
